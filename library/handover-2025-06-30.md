## 4. Detailed Event Reviews

### Event 1: Morning Peak `api-pool` Alerts (2025-06-26)

- **Timing**: 
  - First alert: 2025-06-26 09:18 JST (cleared 09:30 JST)
  - Second alert: 2025-06-26 10:04 JST (cleared 10:15 JST)
- **Service**: `api`
- **Region**: `ap-northeast` (Tokyo)
- **Initial Severity**: P2
- **First Move**: `read logs`

#### Context & Timeline
Thursday morning marks one of the heaviest weekly booking and calendar modification periods in `ap-northeast`. Businesses open their operational dashboards between 09:00 and 10:00 JST, reviewing reservations, updating staff rosters, and confirming schedules while commuters book appointments simultaneously.

At 09:18 JST, the `api-pool` alert rule fired for node `api-apne1-node-03`, indicating connection pool utilization had crossed the 90% threshold for 10 minutes (peaking at 92.4%). 

Because the symptoms showed connection pool saturation rather than an immediate bad deploy (the last `api` deploy was `d-3841`, pushed two days prior on Tuesday afternoon), the first move was **read logs** per Section 3.2. 

Querying `sorrel-prod-*` for `service=api msg="pool exhausted"` showed no hard pool exhaustion errors or dropped incoming requests, but indicated that database connections were being held longer than usual. Cross-referencing the "Sorrel / api" dashboard showed:
- Database query times on primary read replicas ticked up slightly (p95 moving from 45ms to 180ms).
- Primary connection pool utilization was clustered tightly around nodes handling business calendar writes.
- 5xx error rate remained flat (<0.02%), and the monthly SLO error budget panel showed zero material burn.

By 09:30 JST (12 minutes after the alert fired), connection utilization dropped back down to 74% as the 09:00 burst subsided, and the alert cleared automatically.

At 10:04 JST, a second alert fired on node `api-apne1-node-06`, peaking at 91.1% pool capacity. Following the same protocol, I checked `service=api` logs and the database panel. We observed a secondary concurrency burst on slot availability lookups. No pool acquisition timeouts (`msg="pool exhausted"`) occurred. The pool leveled off and cleared at 10:15 JST (11 minutes duration).

#### Outcome & Next Steps
Neither incident degraded booking requests for customers or businesses, and no requests timed out. However, the connection pool headroom during peak Tokyo morning business hours is narrower than comfortable as platform booking volume has grown over the past quarter. 

I discussed the metrics with Ravi (api owner) during working hours on Thursday afternoon. Increasing the connection pool per node requires evaluating database primary memory and client limits across our autoscaling ceiling (up to 20 nodes in `ap-northeast`). Ravi opened an engineering ticket to benchmark and raise the pool allocation per node ahead of the July traffic baseline.
