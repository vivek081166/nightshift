### Details on Follow-Up Tickets

1. **web-4xx threshold tuning on retired help page**
   - **Context:** Early Wednesday morning, an alert fired because the 4xx rate exceeded the threshold for 10 minutes. A quick search in the log search tool (`index=sorrel-prod-*`, query `service=web status>=400 route=*`) showed a cluster of 404 responses targeting `/help/v1/booking-faq`. Support lead Hana confirmed that this help article was retired and unlinked in August, but a third-party directory still crawls the path every few days.
   - **Impact:** Zero customer reports. Legitimate booking traffic, salon dashboards, and admin tools operated normally. Page load latencies remained flat on the "Sorrel / web" dashboard.
   - **Ticket filed:** `PLAT-1842` assigned to Aiko (web owner) to filter out known deprecated documentation paths or tune the alert threshold so that legacy external crawler traffic does not trip `web-4xx` at night when general volume is low.

2. **notifier-queue-depth during nightly reminder batch**
   - **Context:** At 02:40 JST on Saturday, notifier's queue depth passed 1,000 items and remained above that mark for 20 minutes, triggering `notifier-queue-depth`. Inspection of the "Sorrel / notifier" board showed that the scheduler had queued the day's reminder batch for businesses in ap-northeast-1 right on schedule. Delivery metrics confirmed workers were processing at normal speed, and provider outcome panels for Twilio and SendGrid were completely green with no queue stalls or vendor errors.
   - **Impact:** The backlog cleared naturally by 03:45 JST without intervention. Oldest message age remained well within our operating limits.
   - **Ticket filed:** `NOTIF-418` assigned to Tom (notifier owner) to raise the alert window or depth threshold for the 02:00–04:00 JST reminder batch window, reflecting standard operational load as documented in Section 13.1.
